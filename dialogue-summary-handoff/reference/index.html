Only what they got right

The screen shows successes only. Each correct answer is a gold medallion that lands with its own burst and confetti; missed questions are simply not drawn. With no correct answers, a single purple medallion celebrates finishing the dialogue.

HOW IT SCALES

Counted in successes, never in misses

3/3 shows three gold medallions, gold headline and the strongest rays. 2/3 shows two, 1/3 shows one. There is no empty slot, no "1 of 3" and no line about what was missed. Below 3/3 there is a quiet "Try again" button. 0/3 shows one purple "complete" medallion and "Dialogue complete!".

The thinker stays as a small chip. XP, streak and the frame point stay on the next screens. Everything sits inside the shared card and under the shared eyebrow.
