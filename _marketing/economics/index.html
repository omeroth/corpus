FAQ

About economics on Corpus

Is this economics for beginners?

Yes. No math or background is needed. Each dialogue explains one idea in plain language, then shows you the economist's own words.

Which economists will I learn about?

You will meet 30 economists and thinkers, from Adam Smith, Ricardo and Marx to Keynes, Friedman, Hayek, Kahneman and Piketty.

Does it cover today's economy?

Yes. Chapters on behavioral economics, growth and financial crises connect the big ideas to the economy you live in.
