FAQ

About psychology on Corpus

Do I need a psychology background?

No. Every dialogue starts from an everyday question and explains the idea in plain language before you meet the original text.

Which psychologists will I learn about?

You will meet 31 psychologists and thinkers, from Wundt, William James, Freud and Jung to Bowlby, Beck, Frankl and Rogers.

Is this therapy or self-help?

No. Corpus teaches the ideas of psychology and where they came from. It is a way to learn, not a treatment.
