What Brings the Most Happiness?

John Stuart Mill · Ethics · 5-minute dialogue

Today's idea

Today's Idea

Socrates asked questions. Aristotle asked who we should be. Kant asked what we must do. Mill asks a completely different question: What will cause as many people as possible to suffer less and live better?

According to Mill, this is all that ethics should be concerned with.

Think it through

A quick check

According to John Stuart Mill, does this action have moral worth?

A wealthy person donates a vast sum to establish an oncology ward in a hospital. He does so only to gain positive publicity that will help his business, and not out of genuine concern for the patients.

In contrast to Kant, Mill claims that the motive does not change the moral value of the action. The only test is the test of results: did the total happiness in the world increase or did the total suffering decrease? If the donation saved lives, the action is morally right.
