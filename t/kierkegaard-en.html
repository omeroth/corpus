Søren Kierkegaard

Era: 1813–1855

A Danish philosopher, considered the father of existentialism. He contested the habit of presenting faith as a logical conclusion from what is revealed in the world. In "Fear and Trembling" (1843) he argued that faith is a "leap": an act of the individual, not evidence, and that it is precisely when the data do not decide the matter that faith is called for.

In their own words

"Faith is precisely this paradox, that the single individual as the single individual is higher than the universal."
