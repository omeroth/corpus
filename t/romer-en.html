Paul Romer

Era: 1955–

An American economist and Nobel laureate. Answered the question Solow left open, where does technology come from, by placing ideas inside the economic model. Ideas are produced by people responding to incentives, and unlike physical capital they are non-rival: a single formula can serve a million factories at once. Received the Nobel Prize in 2018.

In their own words

"Economic growth occurs whenever people take resources and rearrange them in ways that are more valuable."
