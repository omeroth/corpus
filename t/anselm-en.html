Anselm of Canterbury

Era: 1033–1109

A Benedictine monk and Archbishop of Canterbury. In his "Proslogion" (1078) he formulated what later became known as the "ontological argument": God is "that than which nothing greater can be conceived", and the definition itself requires his existence. The argument was already contested in his own lifetime, and it is still debated today.

In their own words

"Certainly that than which a greater cannot be conceived cannot exist in the understanding alone."
