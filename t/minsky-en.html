Hyman Minsky

Era: 1919–1996

The American economist who argued that financial crises are not external shocks but an internal product of the calm that preceded them. Developed the "financial instability hypothesis" and distinguished between hedge, speculative, and Ponzi finance. Remained on the margins of the mainstream almost all his life; his name returned to the headlines in 2008, when "the Minsky moment" became a common term.

In their own words

"Stability, even of an expansion, is destabilizing in that the more adventuresome financing of investment pays off to the leaders, and others follow."
